Why Reflection

A stockist, not just a shop

We hold real stock, quote the same day, and stand behind every fitting we sell. Whether it's one bulb or a full warehouse retrofit, you get the same answer: fast.

Genuine & Authorised

Every product comes from the authorised channel with full manufacturer warranty, proper billing and a traceable batch. No grey stock, no surprises.

Trade Pricing That Works

Transparent discount on MRP, slab pricing for volume, and project rates for bulk tenders. You see the number, the GST, and the landed cost — upfront.

Real Lighting Advice

Lux levels, beam angles, colour temperature, wattage swaps, driver compatibility. Tell us the space and we'll tell you the right fitting — before you buy.

Stocked & Dispatched

Fast-moving SKUs sit on our shelves, not on a backorder list. Same-day pickup locally, and dispatch across the state for larger orders.

After-Sales You Can Reach

Full Catalogue Access

Solutions

Tell us the space. We'll light it.

Every room has a right answer — the correct lux, the right colour temperature, and a fitting that lasts. Here's where we work most.

Offices & Workspaces

Recessed panels and linear fittings for glare-free, uniform light at desk level. UGR-rated options for screen-heavy floors.

Retail & Showrooms

Track lights, spots and accent downlighters with high CRI — so merchandise colour looks the way it actually is.

Warehouses & Factories

Highbays and lowbays sized to mounting height, with the beam angle picked for aisle width. Built for long hours.

Homes & Apartments

Bulbs, battens, cove and smart lighting. Warm white where you relax, cool white where you work — set up properly.

Streets & Campuses

IP66 street lights and pole fittings with the right pole spacing worked out. Society roads, campuses and parking.

Facades & Outdoors

Floodlights, wall washers and strip for elevations, signage, gardens and gates. Weather-rated, properly aimed.

How It Works

From enquiry to installed

Send your requirement

A room size, a photo of the old fitting, a BOQ, or just "I need 40 panel lights". Any format works — WhatsApp is fastest.

We spec & price it

We confirm the right wattage, colour temperature and beam, then send a written quote with model numbers, rates and GST. Usually within 24 hours.

Confirm & collect

Approve the quote, we raise the invoice and pack the order. Pick up from our counter or we dispatch it to your site.

Supported after

Keep the invoice, keep our number. Warranty claims, add-on quantities and replacements are handled directly by us.
